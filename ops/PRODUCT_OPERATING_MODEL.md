# BVS product operating model (canonical, 2026-08-28)

**Status:** DONE — do not reopen these debates without a new owner decision.

## Artist journey

Create → Rights → Distribute → Earn → See money/status → grow through Radio / BeatStore / services.

## Studio

BVS may be broad. A simple task must not feel broad. Front door is the job (release / beat / service). Full Studio is management.

## iOS

A web deploy must not materially alter the installed iOS product unless explicitly classified iOS-compatible. Term: **BVS iOS-cleared**, never Apple-cleared. Capacitor still loads `https://bvsradio.com/app/ios`.

## Distribution

Distributor is replaceable infrastructure, not BVS’s identity.

## Zimbabwe

BVS should be the musician’s operating system for a fragmented local economy (rights, radio, beats, services, Paynow-shaped money) — not a $24/year DistroKid clone.

## Lyrics Pad

Useful, web-first, low-priority until a genuine purchased-beat buyer uses it. No synthetic analytics.

## Editorial

Music priority first. Targeted evidence second. Human clearance only. No archive mining to rank tracks.
