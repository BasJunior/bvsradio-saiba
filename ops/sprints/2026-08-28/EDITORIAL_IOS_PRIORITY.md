## C. Operating rule

Use this sequence for every future batch:

1. Editorial ranks candidates for **music quality + programming fit**.
2. Editorial selects a small batch (normally 5–10 tracks maximum).
3. BVS checks the selected tracks against the existing Rights Passport/rights metadata.
4. Only selected tracks with a specific evidence gap are handed to M1/VPS/connected-account search for document recovery.
5. Human Editorial/BVS makes the final `cleared` decision in the existing Editorial UI.
6. Stage in beta first and verify `surface=ios` playback/metadata.
7. Production clearance requires explicit approval.

## What M1 / VPS should not do by default

Do not search every Drive/Gmail/archive record merely to rank the catalogue. Evidence recovery becomes an **on-demand support task after Editorial chooses a priority track**.
