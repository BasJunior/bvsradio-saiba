Financial rule

Revenue − artist payout − processor fees − refunds − variable costs − fixed OPEX = real BVS cash flow

Tax collected from buyers is not BVS revenue. GMV is a scale indicator, not profit.

Decision guardrails

  • No product/channel may scale with a negative contribution margin.
  • Use actual Stripe and Paynow fees—not headline rates.
  • Do not deduct placeholder artist withholding without legal confirmation.
  • Do not hire, borrow or value BVS using the optimistic $191,551 NPV alone.
  • Review Base, Conservative and Stress scenarios every quarter.
